## 1. INTRODUCTION

### 1.1 Purpose
This SOP establishes standardized procedures for Trans Link Logistics to ensure consistent, efficient, and compliant freight forwarding operations in Bangladesh.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all operations involving:
- Import/Export clearance (Sea & Air)
- Customs documentation and compliance
- Container handling and warehousing
- Client communication and service delivery
- Financial transactions and invoicing

### 1.3 Regulatory Framework
All operations comply with:
- Bangladesh Customs Act, 1969
- Import Policy Order (current year)
- Export Policy (current year)
- Chittagong Port Authority regulations
- Mongla Port Authority regulations
- Civil Aviation Authority of Bangladesh (CAAB) regulations
- Bangladesh Bank guidelines for foreign exchange

## 2. IMPORT OPERATIONS - SEA FREIGHT

### 2.1 Pre-Arrival Documentation

**Timeline: 7-10 days before vessel arrival**

**Step 1: Receive Shipment Advice**
- Shipper/overseas agent sends shipment advice via email
- Details required: Master B/L number, Container numbers, Gross weight, Number of packages, Description of goods
- Log into system within 2 hours of receipt
- Assign unique job reference number

**Step 2: Obtain Original Documents**
- Request original documents from shipper:
  * Original Bill of Lading (B/L)
  * Commercial Invoice
  * Packing List
  * Certificate of Origin (if applicable)
  * Insurance Certificate/Policy
  * Other certificates (Phytosanitary, Health, Quality certificates as required)
- Verify courier tracking for document arrival
- Update client on document status

**Step 3: Verify Import License & Regulatory Approvals**
- Check if goods require Import Registration Certificate (IRC)
- Verify if restricted/controlled items requiring special permits:
  * BSTI approval (electronics, food items, building materials)
  * Drug Administration approval (pharmaceuticals)
  * Department of Environment clearance (chemicals)
  * Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission (electronics)
- Obtain photocopies of all regulatory approvals
- Maintain approval validity tracking

**Step 4: Pre-Lodge Bill of Entry**
- Access ASYCUDA World system
- Enter consignment details
- Select correct HS Code classification
- Calculate estimated duty and taxes
- Submit for Customs pre-assessment
- Obtain Bill of Entry number

### 2.2 Vessel Arrival & Container Tracking

**Timeline: On vessel arrival day**

**Step 1: Vessel Arrival Notification**
- Monitor Chittagong Port Authority (CPA) website for vessel schedule
- Note actual arrival time and berth allocation
- Update client via email/WhatsApp
- Confirm container discharge schedule

**Step 2: Container Discharge & Positioning**
- Track container discharge from vessel
- Verify container placed in assigned yard
- Check container condition report from CPA
- Report any damages to client and shipping line immediately
- Obtain Delivery Order (D/O) from shipping line:
  * Submit B/L endorsement
  * Pay freight charges (if collect)
  * Pay D/O charges
  * Obtain D/O within 24 hours of vessel discharge

**Step 3: Demurrage & Detention Monitoring**
- Note free time period (typically 4-7 days at port)
- Calculate demurrage start date
- Alert client 2 days before free time expires
- Track container movement to avoid detention charges

### 2.3 Customs Clearance Process

**Timeline: 2-5 working days**

**Step 1: Lodge Bill of Entry**
- Access ASYCUDA World portal
- Complete Bill of Entry with accurate details:
  * Importer's BIN (Business Identification Number)
  * Consignee details
  * Consignment description and HS Code
  * Invoice value in USD
  * Freight and insurance charges
  * Packaging details
- Attach scanned documents:
  * B/L, Invoice, Packing List
  * IRC/Import Permit
  * Insurance certificate
  * Other regulatory certificates
- Submit for Customs assessment
- Pay e-payment portal fees (Processing fee, scanning fee)

**Step 2: Customs Assessment**
- Customs officer reviews submission
- May request physical inspection or documentary check
- If queries raised, respond within same working day
- If valuation dispute, provide supporting documentation:
  * Supplier proforma invoice
  * Market price comparison
  * Previous similar import records
- Obtain assessed Bill of Entry

**Step 3: Duty & Tax Payment**
- Calculate total payable amount:
  * Customs Duty (CD)
  * Supplementary Duty (SD)
  * Value Added Tax (VAT) - 15%
  * Advance Tax (AT)
  * Advance Income Tax (AIT)
  * Regulatory Duty (RD) if applicable
- Pay through any Sonali Bank branch or online portal
- Obtain Treasury Challan/payment receipt
- Upload payment proof to ASYCUDA World
- Wait for Customs release order (typically 2-4 hours)

**Step 4: Physical Inspection (if selected)**
- Coordinate with Customs inspector
- Arrange unstuffing at CPA designated area or Inland Container Depot (ICD)
- C&F representative must be present during inspection
- Prepare goods for 100% examination or sample checking
- Address any discrepancies immediately
- Obtain inspection completion report
- Get release order after satisfactory inspection

**Step 5: Port Clearance & Documentation**
- Submit release order to CPA gate
- Pay port charges:
  * Container handling charges
  * Storage/ground rent
  * Port development fee
  * Jetty charges
- Obtain Equipment Interchange Receipt (EIR)
- Obtain gate pass for container exit

### 2.4 Container Delivery

**Step 1: Arrange Transportation**
- Book covered truck/trailer from approved transporter
- Verify driver has valid license and vehicle fitness
- Ensure vehicle insurance is current
- Provide driver with:
  * Gate pass
  * Delivery Order
  * Container number and seal details
  * Delivery address and contact person

**Step 2: Container Pickup from Port**
- Driver collects container from CPA yard
- Verify seal number matches documentation
- Check container external condition
- Complete EIR with CPA
- Exit port with proper documentation

**Step 3: Delivery to Client**
- Coordinate delivery time with client
- Ensure client representative available for unstuffing
- Verify seal before breaking
- Supervise unstuffing if requested
- Document any cargo damages with photos
- Client signs delivery acknowledgement
- Return empty container to depot within free time

**Step 4: Empty Container Return**
- Transport empty container to shipping line designated depot
- Verify depot location (Pangaon ICD, GMS/SAPL/CCL depot, etc.)
- Obtain empty container return receipt
- Check for any damage charges
- Close container file in system

### 2.5 Post-Clearance Activities

**Step 1: Documentation Filing**
- Scan and upload all documents to digital archive:
  * Original B/L (endorsed)
  * Bill of Entry (assessed)
  * Payment receipts
  * Delivery receipts
  * Correspondence
- Create physical file with photocopies
- Store for minimum 5 years per Customs Act
- Update job status to "Completed"

**Step 2: Client Invoicing**
- Prepare detailed invoice within 2 working days
- Include all charges:
  * Customs duty and taxes
  * Port charges
  * C&F handling charges
  * Transportation
  * Documentation fees
- Attach supporting vouchers
- Email to client accounts department
- Follow up on payment per credit terms

**Step 3: Performance Review**
- Record total clearance time
- Note any delays or issues
- Update client feedback
- File lessons learned for process improvement

## 3. IMPORT OPERATIONS - AIR FREIGHT

### 3.1 Pre-Arrival Procedures

**Timeline: 3-5 days before flight arrival**

**Step 1: Receive Air Waybill (AWB) Details**
- Overseas agent emails AWB copy and cargo details
- Key information: MAWB number, HAWB number (if consolidated), Flight number and date, Gross weight, Number of pieces, Commodity description
- Create job file in system
- Assign job reference number

**Step 2: Obtain Documents**
- Request original AWB (consigned to Trans Link Logistics)
- Receive by courier or airline cargo office
- Obtain commercial invoice, packing list
- Collect regulatory certificates if required
- Verify import permits for restricted items

**Step 3: Pre-Alert Client**
- Notify client of expected arrival
- Request advance payment for duty/taxes if applicable
- Confirm delivery address
- Verify any special handling requirements

### 3.2 Flight Arrival & Cargo Receipt

**Timeline: On flight arrival day**

**Step 1: Monitor Flight Status**
- Check Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (HSIA) cargo system
- Note actual arrival time
- Confirm cargo offloaded from aircraft
- Verify cargo entered into Bangladesh Biman/cargo handler system

**Step 2: Attend Cargo Shed**
- Visit HSIA cargo village within 4 hours of arrival
- Submit AWB original to airline cargo office
- Obtain Cargo Arrival Notice (CAN)
- Pay airline handling charges if collect shipment
- Verify cargo physical location in warehouse

**Step 3: Cargo Inspection**
- Physically verify number of pieces
- Check packaging condition
- Take photos of any damage
- Report discrepancies to airline immediately
- Obtain damage report if applicable

### 3.3 Customs Clearance - Air Import

**Timeline: Same day or next working day**

**Step 1: Lodge Air Bill of Entry**
- Access ASYCUDA World system
- Enter AWB details and cargo description
- Select correct HS Code
- Declare CIF value
- Attach supporting documents digitally
- Submit for assessment

**Step 2: Green/Red Channel Routing**
- System automatically routes based on risk profiling:
  * **Green Channel**: Immediate release, no inspection
  * **Red Channel**: Physical inspection required
- If Red Channel, coordinate inspection time
- Ensure cargo available for examination

**Step 3: Duty Calculation & Payment**
- Review assessed Bill of Entry
- Calculate all applicable duties and taxes
- Make payment via bank or online
- Upload payment proof
- Wait for release order

**Step 4: Customs Inspection (Red Channel)**
- Attend with Customs inspector
- Open packages as directed
- Verify contents match invoice
- Provide additional information if requested
- Obtain release certificate
- Re-pack cargo properly

**Step 5: Cargo Release**
- Submit release order to cargo shed
- Pay storage charges (if applicable, free time: 24-48 hours)
- Obtain delivery order from cargo handler
- Prepare cargo for dispatch

### 3.4 Delivery & Completion

**Step 1: Transportation Arrangement**
- Book suitable vehicle based on cargo size
- Provide driver with delivery documents
- Ensure temperature-controlled vehicle if required
- Verify delivery location and contact person

**Step 2: Cargo Delivery**
- Deliver to client warehouse/factory
- Client signs delivery receipt
- Take delivery photo as proof
- Report any issues immediately

**Step 3: Documentation & Billing**
- File all documents properly
- Prepare client invoice
- Send within 1 working day
- Follow up on payment
- Close job file

## 4. EXPORT OPERATIONS - SEA FREIGHT

### 4.1 Booking & Documentation

**Timeline: 7-14 days before shipment**

**Step 1: Receive Export Inquiry**
- Client provides export details:
  * Commodity description and HS Code
  * Destination port
  * Cargo weight and volume
  * Required shipping date
  * Incoterms (FOB, CFR, CIF, etc.)
- Verify if client has Export Registration Certificate (ERC)
- Check if goods require export permit/license

**Step 2: Obtain Freight Quote**
- Contact shipping lines for rate quotes:
  * Maersk, MSC, Hapag-Lloyd, COSCO, ONE, etc.
- Compare rates for different routes
- Consider transit time and vessel schedule
- Negotiate best rate
- Provide quotation to client with breakdown:
  * Ocean freight
  * Documentation charges
  * Container stuffing charges
  * VGM (Verified Gross Mass) fee
  * Terminal handling charges
  * C&F charges
- Obtain client confirmation

**Step 3: Book Container**
- Confirm booking with shipping line
- Provide:
  * Shipper and consignee details
  * Port of loading: Chittagong/Mongla
  * Port of discharge
  * Container type and quantity (20'/40' Standard/HC)
  * Commodity and weight
  * Expected loading date
- Obtain booking confirmation number
- Receive empty container release order

**Step 4: Arrange Empty Container**
- Collect empty container from depot
- Inspect container condition:
  * No holes or damage
  * Clean and dry
  * Doors open/close properly
  * Valid CSC (Container Safety Convention) plate
- Take photos of interior
- Transport to client factory/warehouse

### 4.2 Cargo Stuffing & Export Documentation

**Timeline: 3-5 days before vessel cutoff**

**Step 1: Container Stuffing**
- Supervise loading at client premises if requested
- Verify cargo matches export documents
- Ensure proper packing to prevent damage
- Load evenly to maintain weight distribution
- Place desiccant bags if moisture-sensitive
- Take photos of stuffing process
- Seal container with high-security seal
- Record seal number
- Prepare VGM certificate

**Step 2: Prepare Export Documents**
- Collect from client:
  * Commercial Invoice (3 copies)
  * Packing List
  * Certificate of Origin (Bangladesh Chamber/EPB)
  * Export LC copy (if applicable)
  * GSP Certificate (if EU/USA shipment)
  * Inspection Certificate (if required)
  * Fumigation Certificate (if wood packaging)
- Verify all details match:
  * Quantities
  * Values
  * HS Codes
  * Consignee information
- Prepare Shipping Bill/Export Form (EXP)

**Step 3: Submit Export Declaration**
- Access ASYCUDA World export module
- Enter Shipping Bill (EXP) details:
  * Exporter ERC number
  * Consignee details
  * Commodity and HS Code
  * FOB value
  * Payment terms
- Attach supporting documents
- Submit to Customs for assessment
- Obtain assessed EXP number

**Step 4: Container Transport to Port**
- Arrange trailer/truck
- Provide driver:
  * Container number and seal
  * EXP form
  * Booking confirmation
  * VGM certificate
  * Port entry permit
- Transport to Chittagong/Mongla Port
- Submit to terminal (CCT, GCB, NCT, etc.)
- Obtain container receipt

### 4.3 Customs Export Clearance

**Timeline: 1-2 days before vessel cutoff**

**Step 1: Customs Examination (if selected)**
- Customs may select for physical inspection
- Coordinate with Customs inspector at port
- Open container if required
- Verify cargo matches declaration
- Re-seal after examination
- Obtain examination report

**Step 2: Export Duty/Tax (if applicable)**
- Most exports are duty-free in Bangladesh
- For restricted items, pay export duty
- Obtain payment receipt
- Upload to ASYCUDA system

**Step 3: Customs Release**
- Customs issues 'Let Export' order
- Container cleared for vessel loading
- Download release certificate
- Forward to terminal operator

**Step 4: VGM Submission**
- Submit Verified Gross Mass to shipping line
- Must be done 24 hours before vessel cutoff
- Provide:
  * Container number
  * Cargo gross weight
  * Container tare weight
  * Total VGM
  * Method used (Method 1 or 2)
  * Authorized signatory

### 4.4 Vessel Loading & Documentation

**Timeline: Vessel sailing day**

**Step 1: Container Loading**
- Terminal loads container onto vessel
- Confirm loading via terminal EDI system
- Obtain loading list/stowage plan
- Note container position on vessel

**Step 2: Obtain Bill of Lading**
- Submit Shipping Instructions to shipping line:
  * Shipper details
  * Consignee/Notify party
  * Port of loading/discharge
  * Description of goods
  * Freight payment terms
  * Number of original B/Ls required
- Verify draft B/L details carefully
- Request corrections if any errors
- Collect original B/Ls (typically 3 originals)
- Types available:
  * Ocean B/L (port-to-port)
  * Multimodal B/L (door-to-door)
  * Telex Release (for full payment terms)
  * Sea Waybill (non-negotiable)

**Step 3: Prepare Export Documents Set**
- Assemble complete documentation:
  * Commercial Invoice (signed & stamped)
  * Packing List
  * Original Bill of Lading
  * Certificate of Origin
  * Export LC/Bill of Exchange (if applicable)
  * Insurance Policy/Certificate (if CIF)
  * GSP/Form A (if applicable)
  * Inspection Certificates
  * Phytosanitary Certificate (agricultural products)
- Make photocopies for file
- Courier originals to:
  * Bank (if LC)
  * Consignee/Agent
  * Exporter for negotiation

**Step 4: Notify Parties**
- Send shipment advice to:
  * Consignee
  * Overseas agent
  * Bank (if LC)
- Include:
  * Vessel name and voyage
  * Bill of Lading number
  * Container number and seal
  * Sailing date and ETA
  * Cargo details
- Track vessel movement
- Update client on vessel progress

### 4.5 Post-Shipment Procedures

**Step 1: Bank Negotiation (LC Shipments)**
- Submit documents to bank within LC validity
- Ensure no discrepancies
- Bank forwards to issuing bank
- Track document acceptance
- Follow up on payment realization

**Step 2: Export Incentives & Proceeds**
- Ensure export proceeds realized within 4 months
- Submit EXP to Bangladesh Bank via exporter
- Apply for cash incentives if eligible:
  * 20% for garments (RMG)
  * 4% for leather goods
  * 2-15% for other sectors
- Follow up on incentive payment

**Step 3: Documentation & Closure**
- File all documents properly
- Prepare client final invoice
- Send statement of account
- Collect payment
- Update job status to completed
- Archive for 5 years

## 5. EXPORT OPERATIONS - AIR FREIGHT

### 5.1 Booking & Preparation

**Timeline: 3-7 days before shipment**

**Step 1: Rate Quote & Booking**
- Contact airlines operating from HSIA/CGIA:
  * Biman Bangladesh, Emirates, Qatar, Turkish, FedEx, DHL, etc.
- Obtain freight rates per kg
- Compare rates and transit times
- Consider:
  * Dimensional weight charges
  * Fuel surcharge
  * Security fees
  * Handling charges
- Confirm booking with airline
- Provide cargo details:
  * Shipper/Consignee
  * Commodity and weight
  * Dimensions (L x W x H)
  * Special handling (DG, Perishable, Live Animals)
  * Flight preference
- Obtain booking reference number

**Step 2: Prepare Export Documents**
- Collect from client same as sea freight:
  * Commercial Invoice
  * Packing List
  * Certificate of Origin
  * Other certificates as required
- Prepare Airway Bill (AWB)
- Lodge Export Declaration (EXP) in ASYCUDA

**Step 3: Cargo Preparation**
- Verify packaging suitable for air transport
- Apply airfreight labels
- Ensure weight and dimensions accurate
- Prepare dangerous goods declaration if applicable

### 5.2 Customs Clearance & Flight Dispatch

**Timeline: 1 day before flight**

**Step 1: Transport to Airport**
- Arrange cargo delivery to HSIA/CGIA cargo village
- Arrive 6-12 hours before flight departure
- Submit to airline cargo acceptance counter
- Obtain cargo receipt

**Step 2: Customs Export Clearance**
- Submit assessed EXP to Customs
- Physical inspection if selected
- Obtain 'Let Export' certificate
- Clear cargo for loading

**Step 3: Security Screening**
- Cargo passes through X-ray/physical screening
- Explosive detection if required
- Apply security seal
- Obtain screening certificate

**Step 4: Flight Loading**
- Airline builds cargo on ULD (Unit Load Device) or bulk
- Loads onto aircraft
- Confirm loading
- Obtain departure notice

**Step 5: AWB Issue & Documentation**
- Collect original Air Waybill from airline
- Obtain Shipper's Delivery Receipt
- Prepare full documents set
- Send to consignee/agent/bank
- Send departure message with:
  * MAWB number
  * Flight number and date
  * Gross weight
  * Number of pieces

### 5.3 Post-Flight Activities

**Step 1: Track Shipment**
- Monitor flight arrival via airline tracking
- Notify consignee of arrival
- Coordinate with destination agent

**Step 2: Documentation & Billing**
- Prepare client invoice
- Submit to accounts
- Follow up on payment
- Close job file

## 6. DOCUMENTATION & RECORD KEEPING

**Import Documents:**
- Original Bill of Lading (B/L) / Air Waybill (AWB)
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Certificate of Origin
- Insurance Certificate/Policy
- Import License/IRC
- Bill of Entry (customs declaration)
- Treasury Challan (duty payment)
- Delivery Order
- Inspection Certificates
- Laboratory Test Reports (if applicable)
- Phytosanitary Certificate
- Health Certificate
- Fumigation Certificate

**Export Documents:**
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Export LC / Contract
- Certificate of Origin (Bangladesh Chamber/EPB)
- GSP Certificate / Form A
- Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
- EXP Form (Customs declaration)
- Inspection Certificate (SGS/Intertek/Bureau Veritas)
- Phytosanitary Certificate
- Health/Veterinary Certificate
- Fumigation Certificate
- Insurance Policy (if CIF/CIP)
- Bill of Exchange

### 6.2 Digital Filing System

**Structure:**
```
Year → Month → Job Number
  ├── 01-Correspondence
  ├── 02-Documents (Invoice, Packing, B/L)
  ├── 03-Customs (EXP/B/E, Assessments)
  ├── 04-Payments (Challans, Receipts)
  ├── 05-Photos (Container, Cargo)
  └── 06-Client Invoice & Delivery
```

**Scanning Standards:**
- Resolution: 300 DPI minimum
- Format: PDF/A for long-term archival
- File naming: JobNo_DocumentType_Date.pdf
- OCR text searchable
- Daily backup to cloud storage

### 6.3 Physical Filing

**Retention Period:**
- Import/Export files: 5 years (Customs Act requirement)
- Financial records: 6 years (Income Tax Ordinance)
- LC documents: 7 years (Bangladesh Bank)
- ERC/IRC copies: Permanent

**Storage:**
- Fireproof cabinet for originals
- Climate-controlled (20-25°C, <60% humidity)
- Access log maintenance
- Annual archive to off-site storage

### 6.4 Document Authentication

**Verification Checklist:**
- [ ] Original stamp/seal present
- [ ] Authorized signatures verified
- [ ] No alterations/erasures
- [ ] Dates consistent across documents
- [ ] Values match (Invoice = B/E = LC)
- [ ] HS Codes accurate
- [ ] Company names spelled correctly
- [ ] Weights and quantities tally

## 7. CUSTOMS CLEARANCE PROCEDURES

### 7.1 ASYCUDA World System Operation

**Login & Access:**
- Each C&F agent assigned unique user ID
- Daily login with password + OTP
- Session timeout: 30 minutes
- Logout after each transaction

**Data Entry Standards:**
- HS Code: Use 8-digit Bangladesh Customs Tariff
- Country Code: ISO 2-letter (BD, CN, IN, US, etc.)
- Currency: 3-letter ISO (USD, EUR, GBP, BDT)
- Unit of Measure: MT, KG, PCS, CTN, etc.
- Values: Round to 2 decimal places
- Zero-error tolerance for BIN, IRC, HS Code

**Common Rejection Reasons:**
- Incorrect HS Code classification
- Mismatch between invoice and B/E values
- Missing IRC/regulatory certificates
- Invalid importer BIN
- Under-valuation detected
- Description too vague

### 7.2 HS Code Classification

**Classification Procedure:**
1. Identify commodity composition and use
2. Refer to Bangladesh Customs Tariff Book (latest edition)
3. Check:
   - Chapter (first 2 digits)
   - Heading (first 4 digits)
   - Sub-heading (6 digits)
   - National tariff (8 digits)
4. Review Explanatory Notes
5. Check for Import Policy restrictions
6. Verify duty rates (CD, SD, VAT, RD)
7. If uncertain, request pre-assessment from Customs

**Critical Sectors:**
- Garments (Chapter 61, 62, 63)
- Textiles (50-60)
- Machinery (84, 85)
- Chemicals (28-38)
- Pharmaceuticals (30)
- Food products (01-24)

### 7.3 Valuation & Assessment

**Valuation Methods (per WTO Agreement):**
1. **Transaction Value** (invoice price) - PRIMARY
2. Transaction value of identical goods
3. Transaction value of similar goods
4. Deductive method
5. Computed method
6. Fall-back method

**Red Flags Triggering Scrutiny:**
- Invoice value significantly lower than market price
- Unusual payment terms
- Related party transactions without adjustment
- Freight charges abnormally low
- Insurance less than 1% of FOB value
- Inconsistent unit prices

**Appeal Process:**
- If assessment disputed, submit written objection within 30 days
- Provide supporting evidence:
  * Proforma invoice from supplier
  * Catalogues/price lists
  * Previous similar import assessments
  * Market research reports
- Request hearing before Deputy/Assistant Commissioner
- If unresolved, appeal to Customs Tribunal

### 7.4 Risk Management & Compliance

**Risk Categories:**
- **Low Risk (Green Channel):** Immediate release, post-clearance audit possible
- **Medium Risk (Yellow Channel):** Documentary review
- **High Risk (Red Channel):** Physical inspection mandatory

**Factors Affecting Risk Profile:**
- Importer compliance history
- Commodity risk level
- Country of origin
- Value threshold
- Regulatory controls
- Intelligence reports

**Compliance Best Practices:**
- Accurate declarations always
- Complete documentation
- Timely responses to queries
- Cooperative during inspections
- Maintain transaction records
- Attend Customs awareness sessions
- Report discrepancies proactively

## 8. CONTAINER & WAREHOUSE MANAGEMENT

### 8.1 Container Handling Procedures

**Empty Container Receipt:**
- Inspect before accepting from depot
- Check ISO container number
- Verify size and type (20'/40' STD/HC/FR/OT/RF)
- Examine for damages:
  * Holes or cracks
  * Rust or corrosion
  * Door functionality
  * Roof integrity
  * Floor condition
- Test door seals and gaskets
- Photograph all four sides + interior
- Report damages to shipping line immediately
- Obtain Equipment Interchange Receipt (EIR)

**Container Stuffing Standards:**
- Clean container interior before loading
- Place heavier items on bottom
- Distribute weight evenly
- Use dunnage to prevent shifting
- Secure cargo with:
  * Wooden bracing
  * Lashing straps
  * Airbags/dunnage bags
  * Shrink wrap
- Leave small gap at door for thermal expansion
- Do not exceed maximum payload:
  * 20' STD: 28-30 MT
  * 40' STD: 26-28 MT
  * 40' HC: 26-28 MT
- Place desiccants if moisture-sensitive cargo
- Close and seal doors securely

**Container Sealing:**
- Use high-security bolt seal
- Record seal number on all documents
- Photograph sealed container
- Never break seal except:
  * At destination with consignee present
  * Under Customs supervision
  * Emergency situation (documented)
- Report seal tampering immediately

**Empty Return:**
- Return within free time (typically 7-14 days)
- Clean interior (sweep/wash)
- Check depot location on shipping line notice
- Obtain empty return receipt
- Verify no damage charges
- File receipt with job documents

### 8.2 Warehouse Operations

**Inward Cargo Receipt:**
- Check delivery against advice note
- Count packages/pieces
- Verify packaging condition
- Record gross weight
- Note any damages with photos
- Issue Goods Receipt Note (GRN)
- Assign storage location
- Update inventory system
- Notify client of receipt

**Storage Standards:**
- Temperature: 20-30°C ambient
- Humidity: <60% RH
- Ventilation: Adequate airflow
- Pest control: Monthly treatment
- Fire safety: Extinguishers every 15m, smoke detectors
- Security: CCTV, access control, security guard
- Lighting: Minimum 200 lux
- Stacking height: Maximum 3m or as per package marking

**Inventory Management:**
- Daily stock verification
- FIFO (First In First Out) principle
- Bin location system
- Weekly cycle counting
- Monthly full inventory
- Reconcile with ERP system
- Report discrepancies within 24 hours

**Outward Dispatch:**
- Receive delivery instruction from client
- Pick goods from bin location
- Verify quantity and condition
- Load onto vehicle under supervision
- Client/transporter signs delivery note
- Update inventory system
- File proof of delivery

### 8.3 Special Cargo Handling

**Temperature-Controlled Goods:**
- Maintain cold chain integrity
- Use reefer containers or insulated trucks
- Monitor temperature continuously
- Record temperature logs
- Immediate delivery post-clearance
- Examples: Pharmaceuticals, perishable food, vaccines

**Dangerous Goods (DG):**
- Identify DG class (1-9 per IMDG/IATA)
- Obtain Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS)
- Use appropriate packaging and labeling
- Segregate from incompatible cargo
- Train staff on DG handling
- Maintain emergency response kit
- Notify authorities as required
- Examples: Chemicals, batteries, aerosols, flammables

**Fragile/High-Value:**
- Special handling "This Side Up", "Fragile"
- Extra cushioning/padding
- Secure storage area
- Insurance coverage verification
- Photo documentation
- Delivery with signature
- Examples: Electronics, glassware, artwork, jewelry

**Oversized/Heavy Cargo:**
- Assess lifting equipment needs
- Plan route for transportation
- Obtain road permits if required
- Use flatbed/lowbed trailer
- Hire experienced crew
- Examples: Machinery, transformers, boilers, generators

## 9. FINANCIAL PROCEDURES

### 9.1 Cost Estimation & Quotation

**Import Quotation Components:**
1. **Customs Duty & Taxes:**
   - Customs Duty (CD): 0-25% on CIF
   - Supplementary Duty (SD): 0-500% on (CIF+CD)
   - Value Added Tax (VAT): 15% on (CIF+CD+SD)
   - Advance Tax (AT): 5% on (CIF+CD+SD)
   - Advance Income Tax (AIT): 2-5% on (CIF+CD+SD)
   - Regulatory Duty (RD): 0-100% on CIF (if applicable)
   
2. **Port Charges (Chittagong Port):**
   - Container Handling: ~BDT 10,000-15,000 per TEU
   - Port Development Fee: ~BDT 3,000 per TEU
   - Ground Rent: BDT 500-1,000 per day after free time
   - Jetty Charges: BDT 2,000-3,000 per TEU
   - Scanning Fee: BDT 2,000 per container
   
3. **Shipping Line Charges:**
   - Delivery Order (D/O): ~USD 50-150
   - Documentation Fee: ~USD 30-50
   - B/L Surrender (if applicable): ~USD 50
   - Demurrage: ~USD 75-150 per day per container
   - Detention: ~USD 75-150 per day per container
   
4. **C&F Service Charges:**
   - Handling charge: BDT 8,000-15,000 per container
   - Documentation: BDT 2,000-5,000
   - Petty cash: BDT 1,000-2,000
   - Transport to client: As per km/location
   - Unstuffing supervision: BDT 2,000-3,000
   
5. **Other Costs:**
   - Bank charges: 0.2-0.3% of invoice value
   - Insurance: 0.1-0.5% of CIF value
   - Survey fees (if inspection): USD 100-300
   - Laboratory test: BDT 5,000-20,000

**Export Quotation Components:**
1. **Ocean Freight:**
   - Rate per container/per CBM/per RT
   - Bunker Adjustment Factor (BAF)
   - Currency Adjustment Factor (CAF)
   - Port Congestion Surcharge (if applicable)
   - Peak Season Surcharge (if applicable)
   
2. **Local Charges:**
   - Terminal Handling: ~USD 100-150 per container
   - VGM Fee: ~BDT 500-1,000
   - Documentation: ~BDT 2,000-3,000
   - Export clearance: BDT 5,000-8,000
   - Stuffing supervision: BDT 2,000-3,000
   - Transport to port: As per distance
   
3. **Certificate Charges:**
   - Certificate of Origin: BDT 500-1,000
   - GSP Certificate: BDT 1,500-2,500
   - Inspection Certificate: USD 100-300
   - Phytosanitary: BDT 2,000-5,000

**Quotation Preparation:**

### 11.2 Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & KYC

**Know Your Customer (KYC):**
- Collect client documents before first transaction:
  * Trade License copy
  * VAT Registration
  * TIN Certificate
  * IRC/ERC
  * BIN (Business Identification Number)
  * Directors' NID copies
  * Company incorporation certificate
  * Board Resolution (for authorization)
- Verify authenticity
- Update annually
- Maintain in client master file

**AML Red Flags:**
- Unusually large cash payments
- Reluctance to provide information
- Frequent changes in consignee
- Vague description of goods
- Under-valuation or over-valuation
- Sanctioned countries involvement
- High-risk commodities (gold, precious stones, dual-use items)

**Suspicious Transaction Reporting:**
- Report to Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit (BFIU)
- Within 7 days of suspicion
- Maintain confidentiality
- Do not inform client
- Keep detailed records
- Cooperation with authorities

**Restricted/Prohibited Items:**
- **Prohibited for Import:** Narcotic drugs, used vehicles (except vintage), obscene materials, certain chemicals, etc.
- **Prohibited for Export:** Archaeological items, antiques, wildlife, etc.
- **Restricted Items:** Require special permission/license
- Maintain updated list from Import/Export Policy
- Verify client compliance
- Refuse suspicious shipments
